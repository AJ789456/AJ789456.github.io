ShoxBox

The ShoxBox was a fun game I made in my Practicum in STEM class. In this game, the player presses the two foil-covered pads on the sides of the machine and receives a shock. If they are able to keep their hands on the pads, they progress to the next level, where they receive a stronger shock. This continues until they flinch and remove their hands from the contact pads. The brain of the game was an Arduino that I programmed to control some LEDs and a separate circuit board that was in charge of generating the shocks. The LEDs indicated the level to the user, and the shocks were created using a step-up transformer on the secondary circuit board.
