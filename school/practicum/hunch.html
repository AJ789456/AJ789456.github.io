ID Holder Multi-tool

NASA HUNCH Design and Prototype

Allen ISD STEAM Center, Allen, TX

Avanish Jeendru


NASA employees will no longer have to go search for simple tools thanks to the ID Holder Multi-tool. This tool provides them with quick access to frequently used tools. All models were designed in Autodesk Fusion and 3D printed on a Creality K1.


Failures and Redos:

Generation 1:

The 1st generation prototype had a ruler with messy numbers, a flimsy screwdriver and a slot that couldn't hold an ID. It was completely useless.

Generation 2:

The 2nd generation prototype also had a flimsy screwdriver, but the slot could hold an ID (if it had been wider), and the numbers of the ruler looked good. This generation showed me that I had to make drastic changes to the design to include more tools and better tools.

Generation 3:

The 3rd generation prototype was a complete redesign. It had a laser pointer, bit-holder, pencil-holder, and 2 usable rulers. This came at the cost of weight and thickness, but I decided it was an acceptable compromise. The ID holder was split into 3 parts which was unnecessary.

Generation 4:

The 4th generation prototype was similar to the 3rd generation, just slightly more refined. The ID holder was split into 2 parts instead of 3 and now included a smartphone stand. I also improved the laser pointer in this generation by making the button easier to press and by making the laser easier to assemble through the integration of a wiring channel.

Generation 5:

The 5th generation prototype made me think about how to improve the consumer experience. I decided that the main goals were making the ID holder thinner and packing another tool into design. In the end, I was able to make the ID holder 10% thinner and add a flip-out USB drive for digital storage.
